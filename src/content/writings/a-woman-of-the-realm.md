*We conversed with **[Zipporah Banyay](https://substack.com/@zipporahbanyay)** regarding her ethereal chronicles. She’s an Author, Podcast Host, Energy Healer, and Intuitive Healing Guide with a love of healing and ancestry. We appreciate the opportunity to highlight her journey.*

*“Strong, Rooted, Spiritual”*

## When did your spiritual journey begin?

> > I would say that my spiritual journey began the day I was born because I truly believe that everything that happened to me good and bad built me into who I am today. My conscious spiritual journey began at a very rough phase of my life. I was at a crossroads and I was headed for self-destruction, my choices were life or death and I was blessed to be able to meet a therapist that introduced me to meditation and yoga, this was almost 2 decades ago.

## How did that moment affect the person you are today?

> > That moment changed my life in ways I could have never imagined or perceived at the time. When I experienced meditation for the first time it got me outside of my body and into my spirit realm. I became a seeker of all things spiritual.

## What aspects of life do you genuinely find enjoyable?

> > I enjoy connecting with people on a spiritual level. I enjoy having conversations and connections that vibrate higher than the physical 3-Dimensional reality. I enjoy using the energy of creativity to express my many expressions of divinity. I enjoy anything that pleases the senses, all six of them. If it sounds good, I love it, if it taste good it’s delicious, if it feels good, I feel it deep, If it looks good, I want to see more. If it taps my spirit, I experience it fuller. The most enjoyable experience of all is living a full life and enjoying the many gifts from God.

## Who are the individuals who inspire you the most, and what are their backgrounds like?

> > I would say a Iyanla Vanzant and My great grandmother, Nettie Brantley.
> 
> > Iyanla Vanzant is an American inspirational speaker, lawyer, New Thought spiritual teacher, author, life coach, and television personality. She is known primarily for her books, her talk show, and her appearances on The Oprah Winfrey Show.
> 
> > I enjoy the strength of a Iyanla, Her spiritual path and her ability to overcome trauma in such a tremendous way. She is the embodiment of a great mother to many and she is the type of woman I aspire to be in my elder years.
> 
> > My great grandmother Nettie Brantley was my first example and experience of peace amongst chaos. Her ability to remain calm, centered, and in a world all her own was an inspiration to me. She made such an impact in my life that long after her transition in 1992 I kept her close and near as a very dear ancestor in my heart. I aspire to reach and maintain that level of peace as an elder woman.
> 
> ![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/bc2b9de4-13b8-4f4c-bd33-85d6213372aa_1024x1792.jpeg)

## How do you receive downloads?

> > I receive spiritual downloads in a variety of ways. Sometimes it’s an intuitive matter of fact all knowingness, sometimes it’s a burst of telepathic information that is so detailed and descriptive and deep that the information is only received once and I internalize it. It never gets repeated it just becomes encoded in me. Sometimes it’s an activation, almost like turning the light switch on inside of me and I began to access information deep in my DNA. My dream downloads are the most interesting, sometimes I remember the dream, sometimes I wake up with no recollection of the dream, but an all knowing this about what to do next.

## Are you satisfied with the lifestyle you have created for yourself?

> > I am satisfied with where I am today, but there’s always room for a growth and development. Knowing where I came from and what I’ve overcome is, what gives me the satisfaction, but I’m learning new things about myself daily and I hope to never stop learning and to always remain teachable. That’s why I enjoy deep spirit connections so much because it’s fascinating to me, who we are and what we have to share with one another when we connect spirit to spirit. Jealousy, hating and anger don’t function on that vibrational level.

## Can you tell us a bit about your background and how you became interested in tarot reading?

> > When I tapped into meditation, it opened up a portal and another world for me, and I began to seek things all spirit. I went from meditation to crystals to metaphysics, and I was always trying to communicate with other dimensions and spiritual realms. I always knew that there was more than just human beings on earth swirling around in the galaxy of a vast universe. I wanted clarity in my own life, and I kept the practice up all these years and when there was confusion in my life, I would pick them back up and get the messages that would help guide my intuition. I did a few readings here and there for friends, but I just recently started divining tarot for other people publicly.

## How long have you been practicing tarot, and what drew you to this particular form of divination?

> > I’ve been practicing tarot for over 15 years, but I’ve practiced the most in the last three years. I was drawn to this particular practice of divination because it worked right away. I’ve always been a show me girl, and the tarot has helped me throughout the years gain clarity during times of confusion. In a lot of my spiritual journey and experience with various cultures, most of them used some sort of tarot divination so I was always around it. Whether I was practicing or whether I was getting a reading.
> 
> ![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/9dcadd2d-84f2-474e-919f-cc525f17ea18_1255x717.jpeg)

## How would you describe your approach to tarot reading? Do you follow a specific tradition or style?

> > I think in the beginning, it’s important to have some sort of practice to follow just to get used to working with your deck, but eventually once you start to work with your spirit guides and ancestors, you will customize your own approach and means of communicating with the cards. That’s what happened for me, The main thing my spirit guides and ancestors wanted me to get down and there was no compromise about this was a proper opening and closing. When working with the tarot I’m literally opening portals and the last thing you want to do is leave portals or spiritual doors wide open for any and everything to come through. The opening is extremely important to make sure only the things, I invite to come through. I work very closely with my ancestors, and so my opening always consist of a prayer and and invocation.

## How do you handle situations where a reading might reveal something potentially troubling or unexpected?

> > If it’s a personal reading for myself, I’ve been doing this long enough to know never to disregard any messages. They all have a important message for me. In the beginning when I got a card, I didn’t want to see I would keep throwing the cards expecting a different message lol, but I would just get the same message in a different card. Most of the readings that I’ve done for other people are usually messages that ignite something in the other person that encourages them to use their intuition, spirit guides or ancestors and the number one response I usually get is “I knew that or I felt that.” It’s not necessarily for me to be the bearer of bad news, I’m simply a conduit to pass the message or the warning. Whether the person believes it or not is up to them, their experience will tell in time.
> 
> ![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/1004b063-05de-48a4-906e-231ddf9c1c82_1024x1792.jpeg)

## In your opinion, what are some common misconceptions about tarot reading and tarot readers?

> > I would say the biggest misconception about tarot reading is that it’s demonic, and that tarot readers don’t believe in God. I hope I don’t step on the toes of anybody with these next statements. Anything can be demonic and to believe in demons, and not believe in the spirit realm having other spirit bodies and entities is a contradiction. To believe in the Holy Spirit and not believe that there’s other spiritual beings in other dimensions is delusional. To believe that heaven is the only spiritual realm that our ancestors and loved ones go to is spiritually immature. I believe in God and All of the tarot readers that I know, believe in some great spirit of the universe, if you’re gonna be opening spiritual portals and doors, you want to be on the God side, not the demon side, lol. In everything there is a duality ie: light and dark, good and evil, happy and sad, God and the devil they all exist. I don’t pick and choose the parts that make me feel comfortable. I’d rather be aware of what’s really going on so that I can protect myself accordingly. During my opening of any tarot reading, I always incorporate not only my ancestors and spirit guides, but also God, I believe in God and the great spirit of the universe based on my personal relationship with this divine source and not out of religiosity. I stay connected.

## How can you merge all your skills into a profitable venture?

> > That’s a great question. The best part about accessing all of me as a fully expressing spirit being, who has a database of wisdom and talents is that I have many avenues that I could turn into a profitable venture. The key here is making sure that I’m staying true to purpose and that my venture has the number one motive of helping other people. Me being who I am, my spirit returned. A big part of my purpose is helping other people to tap into their divine selves through healing, and connection with their ancestors, and from there, the individual goes on the journey of their life purpose. I can’t get caught up in the outcome for another individual because everybody’s journey is their own. Currently some things that I’m doing is I have a podcast, I just recently wrote a book The Five Stages of Shadow Work and I’m looking forward to building community with like-minded individuals, to help more people on their healing journey. I offer spiritual consultations and tarot readings and I have an online boutique Goddess Expressions Boutique LLC. Some of the things things you will see from me soon are live classes, as well as courses, more books, and eventually retreats with other healers and spiritualists, offering a buffet of spiritual practices and rituals that usher people on their healing journey.

## Do you have any guidance for someone who admires you?

> > Awe, I would say we are all unique spirits having a human experience, I’m just one of them. Tap into your unique gifts and start sharing them with the world. There are many people who need exactly what you have to offer. I can’t wait to see who you become. ❤️
