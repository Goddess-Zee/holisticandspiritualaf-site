Grand rising beautiful souls. I had absolutely no intention of writing today and then was flooded with emotions that were bursting out of me and urging me to put this on paper so here I am, sometimes I find the most impromptu writings are the best. I’ve had a lot of internal changes coming to me strongly since about June of this year, and I’ve been very transparent about those changes in my previous writings. As a matter of fact, I’m extremely vulnerable for the traditional social media superficiality, which brings me to the point of this writing. Lately, my ancestors and spirit guides have been ushering me rather hurriedly in alignment with my true self and it’s no surprise that on November 19th we officially entered the age of Aquarius marked by Pluto, entering Aquarius, and out of Capricorn. I don’t know about you, but the last 14 years for me were a trudge until about 2 1/2 years ago. I kicked off those 14 years having just lost my husband to his battle with cancer and I was thrust into being a single mother and life took some dips, twists, turns, and pivots along the way all while finding myself and healing generational trauma. It was some of the hardest times of my life mixed in with some of the most transformational times of my life. There were lots of what’s the points and plenty of ”get up you can do it, you’re so close!” from my ancestors and spirit guides. There were times I wanted to flat-out give up, but the inner flame inside of me kept pushing, and sometimes I was being carried solely by the wind and the energy of my ancestors.

I say all that to say it’s a new cycle, baby, and I’m here for it. I know intuitively my life will never be a struggle again, but that doesn’t mean, life won’t be lifin and showing up in all kinds of mysterious ways like it tends to do. It just means that I ain’t trudging no more. I’ve mastered some lessons, healed some karmic cycles, fulfilled a portion of my purpose, and effectively healed generational trauma in myself and it’s now billowing out to my family and I am alive to see and experience the transformation in them. Such a beautiful experience life is today and how serendipitously different it is. As a little girl, I often wondered why I was afforded a life of struggle, pain, and anguish. At that time I could never imagine I would grow up to be grateful for every part of it. That’s healing! We’re no longer trauma bonding but seeking out healthy and positive relationships only.

Now that I have successfully completed that part of my life’s purpose I’m now moving into the next phase of my journey and purpose and I must admit it’s like living two lives in one because I feel like I’m starting all over again, but from a new foundation if that makes any sense. It’s thrilling, and maddening at the same time. Sometimes I feel like wow I learned all that just to return to a place where I was riddled with lack of clarity again. Meaning, I don’t have clarity as to which direction this journey will take me, but I have learned to trust the process and to be obedient along the way, I know that my ancestors and spirit guides have never let me down and have always seen me through things that I could’ve never imagined I’d experience. So I trust today.

I receive downloads and messages constantly, and one message that I hear very clearly is to be mindful of my energy exchanges. I recently made a TikTok video on this.

**[Check it out here](https://www.tiktok.com/@holisticandspiritualaf/video/7442067379892276510?is_from_webapp=1&sender_device=pc&web_id=7439108962748712478)**

I’m feeling a strong urge to cut out relationships that don’t serve my higher ascension. If it’s not an alignment, it has to go and this goes for all areas of my life. Anything or anyone who does not support raising consciousness I simply cannot spend too much time entertaining anymore. It’s a total energy drain and my energy is too precious. My ancestors also urged me to receive the proper energy exchange for my energy output. I can recall the days when I would not charge for classes, I would give my time freely to people with no boundaries and not get the same in return. Those days are over. My focus and goal now are helping to raise the consciousness on the planet as we embark on this exciting journey of a new world and consciousness as souls having a human experience. This is what my soul was preparing for with all of the healing I encountered and now I’m in a position to join the other light workers, star seeds, nine ether beings, indigo children, etc… on our soul’s mission.

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/87573f57-158e-4b6b-95ea-4eb659758bce_631x634.png)

In 2022 my ancestors urged me to start a podcast, I had no idea what or why I was doing it, but I’m grateful that I did. It started as a personal experience podcast. And recently grew into interviews with other healers, sharing their experiences and gifts. The connections I’ve been making with these spirits are no accident. I realize that together we are making an effort to assist in raising the consciousness. One of my greatest fears was sharing my spiritual gifts with the world out of fear. I had this weird sort of imposter syndrome, I’ve been investigating this recently and I’m sure this is connected to some past life experience. That’s not that uncommon at all amongst energy workers and healers. I was urged by my spirit guides and ancestors to get out there and shine my light. In the process of doing this, I recognized certain people were not pleased with my transition, and this led to the understanding that not everybody’s coming along for this phase of the journey. Recently I incorporated regular master classes to help these awakened souls that are wanting to know how to ready their physical bodies to receive the downloads that are coming fast and heavy. As people are being removed, certain people are being attracted to me and what I have to offer. My writing ability is a latent talent that laid dormant in my DNA up until about a year and a half ago. I wrote a piece on this.

***[Latent Talents in my DNA](https://medium.com/holistic-and-spiritual-af/latent-talents-in-my-dna-8c219557d91f)***

My writing started out similar to my podcast. It was my personal experience and journey from healing trauma to empowerment. I started writing originally as inspirational and introspective quotes, and then it grew into my gift of storytelling. I quickly found that some people carried the energy of jealousy, as if I shouldn’t be able to do those types of things and I distanced myself from those people and took my writings for eyes that could see. I went on to write a book ***[The Five Stages of Shadow Work](https://www.amazon.com/Stages-Shadow-Work-Zipporah-Banyay/dp/B0D57DR7GX/ref=sr_1_1?crid=11HB8HR71SM7T&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.YzdUOb-QU5h5Md0QBbHOpwdfiWbEbBWkcgre_2VGrms.uMpxM490SUO_hW-IriqgoKu45c9EtHTi9Svglbs-Mhg&dib_tag=se&keywords=the+five+stages+of+shadow+work&qid=1733359743&sprefix=the+five+stages+of+shadow+work%2Caps%2C122&sr=8-1)*** that was inspired by a podcast episode. I continued to write and my writing got better as I grew in the development of my skill. I once again ran across certain individuals who were intimidated by my gift and what I would later find is lots of people reading my writings, but not commenting, liking, or really appreciating the energy exchange. Now I get that not everyone is gonna like my style of writing or my topics and that’s OK. I only write to reach the people meant to receive the message, that being said, my ancestors have urged me to be properly compensated for my writings to ensure that these writings will reach the right persons. Whether that’s money or simply appreciation, no wasted energy here. I am worthy to receive all the blessings coming my way I’m looking forward to a fabulous and magical next 20-year cycle under the umbrella of raised consciousness across the planet, and I will do my part and fulfill my mission in my lifetime.

This is the main reason that I have such a beautiful relationship with my ancestors because, throughout all of this, I have cried, stomped my feet, and been defiant but also I have grown and transformed into the beautiful person I am today, always and forever guided by the wisdom of my ancestors, they have never let me down. I know this isn’t everyone’s journey but I am grateful for the role they have played in my life getting me to the point of self-actualization and returning me to source energy personified. My words and stories are spoken from my spirit and experience as an ancestor returned guided by the ancient ones who came before me.
