*When is it ever OK to not be OK?* 

Why is it that we have an automatic reply when people ask generically “How ya doing?” It’s like an emotional response that immediately blocks the authentic feelings and presents a mask of. “I’m OK.” whether we are or not. It’s so automatic and ingrained in the subconscious to not let people into what’s truly going on for fear of what they may think of us or how we may be judged. 

Where the hell did that come from?

For me, I can honestly say that my trauma patterns have caused me to not trust people a long time ago. I always felt I couldn’t trust people because if the people closest to you will hurt you what will the people who are not close to you do? I learned that as a little girl.  Fear has sometimes kept me trapped in judgment responses from unhealed people who would say things like “You should’ve known better”, or “You shouldn’t have done that”, as if experiencing the lesson and learning from it organically isn’t enough. I got that one from childhood too. And my favorite, sometimes when you decide to show up authentically be honest and open-hearted, and tell people things aren’t OK you get this look and generic response like *I didn’t really give a damn how you were doing. I was just giving the illusion that I cared* or even worse judgment. This usually leaves me feeling foolish for opening up and reinforcing my normal response of not trusting people.

I had this experience the other day and it made me reflect on all of this.

It’s been a wild couple of weeks. Lots of energetic movement some pleasant, some not so much. Throughout it all has been a whirlwind of emotions of ups and downs sadness, joy, anger, and fear. Bouncing around like a ball on a ping-pong table. Honestly, it’s all been rather draining to my energy supply. I work on my energy body regularly but it’s still very possible for me to get drained by so many complex emotions all at the same time and the fact that we’re in a mercury retrograde doesn’t help. There are times when all the cleansing and clearing rituals can’t stop what needs to be felt for growth. So when I was asked by someone, “How are you doing?”  I put on my Chester cheese grin and hit the automatic reply of “I’m OK. My inner compass gently nudged me to say to me “No you’re not, you need to process these complex emotions or they will erode you and turn into a hole in your soul.” (*Damn intuition, is on point every time and those ancestors don’t miss nothing)* I know from experience it’s best to deal with them as they come or I’ll be doing the shadow work later. 

So I get to business, processing emotions which is such a daunting task. I’d rather busy myself with distractions so that I don’t have to deal with it but I know today that’s not an option for me. It is but it’s not the healed response. It’s more of the trauma response and that leads to a temporary feeling of false peace. So I get to talk about it with a trusted ear. I am truly blessed to have that because that makes a world of difference. I believe that having someone or some bodies in your life that you get to take off all the masks and share your inner going-ons in a judgement-free zone is essential to getting through stuff without stuffing and repressing unprocessed emotions. So, I get to vent to them, my ancestors, and spirit guides and *then* I have to start processing the emotions of these feelings one by one alone for as long as it takes to get to the other side. Mercury retrograde is actually a perfect time for this type of inner work and this writing is helping to process the muck too.


I’m still in my feelings because things are still happening in me, but I have to trust the process and know that everything is balancing the scales in the universe. That sucks too because I want to manifest some shit and make things go my way but instead, I have to trust that things are happening exactly the way they’re supposed to and sit with my feelings. Talk about emotional maturity. 

What’s that?  I want to be the kid that didn’t get what she wanted. 

Who am I or better yet? Who am I becoming? 

As uncomfortable as I am today, I’m grateful to have the wisdom and understanding that these feelings will pass and for now I’ll continue to grow and allow space and grace for myself to transform. If you’re going through some shit or better yet growing through some things and you feel not OK. Know that it’s OK to not be OK, this too will pass, and trust the process.
