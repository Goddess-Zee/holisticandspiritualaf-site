The other day I had a dream and in my dream, there were familiar faces, of people I hadn’t encountered since high school. In this dream, I was with a woman who is like a mother figure to me. She was laying in a hospital bed and we were all surrounding her being in a hospital bed and this surprised me because she’s a very active woman with plenty of life to live. I was shocked to see the two girls from high school, but it seemed like our relationship was recently familiar. There were two other ladies there that I consider as Sisters, we share a common relationship with the mother figure but we don’t see each other often. At the start of the dream, I was watching the screen as if watching a movie and then I phased into the scene. I woke up like what the hell was that about?

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/2bfb457d-0bdb-4e1a-80c1-765081182132_1024x1024.webp)

About two weeks before that, I had a dream about an ex-best friend. This one had me in tears, he and I had been friends since the latter part of middle school up until a pivotal and traumatic point in my life when I needed him the most, he had become engaged and I guess his fiancé didn’t appreciate our close friendship so quite naturally he chose her over our friendship. This crushed me because his timing was so off. If I ever needed him in our friendship, it was at that moment, in this period of my life where I was navigating some intense grief. I could tell it crushed him too, but he had to keep peace in his house. I get it! I didn’t like it but I got it. I woke up and tears gently streamed down my face, but the intensity of the pain was deep as if I was reaching the innermost parts of myself. I meditated for a while on what exactly those dream messages were for me. 

The last few weeks have been very reflective for me lately, and these dreams are just a part of it. I left a toxic job and chose peace and this has given me some downtime to get back into my inner realm and what’s most important to me. During the Lionsgate portal and the mercury retrograde in August, I felt a significant shift in my energy body and I answered the call to tap back into meditation, yoga, detoxes, tea rituals, etc... I’ve even included more nature and vitamin D and this has brought about immense changes in my DNA and my frequency is shifting. I’m attracting people to me, but not just random people, kindred spirits who are in direct alignment with my purpose. I am with spiritualists, empaths, energy healers, herbalists, and metaphysicians and it feels so good. I have the remainder of the year podcast guests recorded and we are doing amazing work for humanity. I’m eating better and resting properly and this is all wearing well on me. 

I remember when I was preparing to leave the job mentally and I ran it by people and they would say oh well it’s a job don’t leave until you get something else. Never mind it was taxing my spirit and draining the hell out of my energy. The very essence of me was being depleted by this toxicity. I heeded their warnings and stayed a little longer but I could no longer ignore the magnetic pull inside of me or my intuition to return to my natural state of being.

For most of my life, I’ve been an entrepreneur. I’ve had a few jobs here and there along the way I have lots of hirable skills and I’m at an age where a company would hire me because of my maturity and responsibility over my younger peers. My time as an entrepreneur, was perfect for me because I was always able to move and flow with what was happening with me internally, and it afforded me the ability to be able to take care of my spiritual energy body first before dealing with the physical aspects of me, there was plenty of time for self-love and self-care, and being there for more daughters while they were growing up and I’m so grateful for that today because I know it was instrumental to my spiritual growth throughout the years.

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/cc8f53af-c130-4526-b9ae-1cc94b77db31_1024x1024.webp)

Now back to the present time and I’m going through another growth spurt and I’m seeing why, it was important for me to follow my own intuition and leave that job because it nudged me back over where I belong and in alignment with my purpose. So I find myself going through another growth spurt, and I’m here for it and fully present. Those tools and rituals that I have recharged inside of myself have raised my frequency and alignment to the fifth-dimensional reality that we’re headed in as humanity. So of course, my dreams are more charged and closer to the surface. I’m just grateful I heard the call from my ancestors when they told me that anything that was not in alignment with my purpose had to be removed, including a relationship. My obedience to spirit saves me again and I’m off like the major arcana Fool of the tarot on a new journey of greater understanding. The interesting part about heading into this new world and a higher level of consciousness is I’ve been time traveling, revisited by my past experiences throughout my life, it’s as if my life is flashing before my eyes daily, bits and pieces, chunks by chunk, and putting all the pieces together that created me today.

I’m fascinated by yet another consciousness expansion on my soul’s journey. Things deep inside my DNA that I always knew since I was a little girl being validated and confirmed in the exact journey that I experienced in my life. This next chapter will be nothing less than magnificent. I can feel it in every fiber of my being as the tables of power get returned to the chosen ones I take my place and express my gratitude for being here at such a magnificent time to be on earth.

The journey continues and so does my evolution.
