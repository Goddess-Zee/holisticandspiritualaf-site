That’s exactly what transitioning from 3rd density to 5th density feels like. For some people this will sound unbelievable but for those going through the shift it will resonate and that’s who this is for. At the beginning of the year I spoke of this being a 9 year and time for transformation.

Well now we are in September and in a Saturn retrograde in Pisces. With the patriarchal time construct going faster than the speed of light we’re now at the biggest transformation of all. This shit just got do or get left behind. I recently had a podcast episode guest and we talked about what will happen to the souls that refuse to learn the lessons. We didn’t actually record this part, this was the after show convo which sometimes gets more spicy than the actual episode, his interpretation was that the soul will leave the body in 3D and live in 5D leaving the uninitiated to continue to go through their karmic lessons until they actually transform. Leaving them stuck as a npc (non player character) and this consists of living life on autopilot, in the program without any real control or accountability of ones life. That’s some heavy consequences!

So how do we avoid that?

I know you’ve heard folks say it “I’m sovereign, I’m sovereign” but sovereignty is not just a trendy word. It’s not a vibe you slap on your IG bio. Sovereignty is a lived embodiment. It’s the way you move, the way you love, the way you speak, and the way you protect your energy. It’s choosing your life with intention instead of reacting from trauma, programming, or fear.

To be sovereign means self-ownership. It means you’re aligned with your soul, not living on autopilot, not just going along with what the world tells you to do, but choosing with your whole spirit. And yes this sounds beautiful, but it’s also layered. It takes practice. It takes unlearning. And it takes spiritual maintenance.

I want to share five powerful ways to begin living sovereignly rooted in divine feminine energy, ancestral healing, and deep soul alignment.

With that being said, let’s get into the how of it! How can you start to embody sovereignty and not just regurgitate the trending term on social media. I’m gonna break this down in 5 ways but I’m sure there’s others, like I said be aware of your own inner compass.

**1\. Come Home to Your Body**

Your body is an altar. It carries wisdom, signals, and intuition that are constantly guiding you. When your solar plexus tightens around someone new, when your womb stirs, when your ears ring your body is speaking. Sovereignty begins with listening.

Emotional mastery is part of this. Are you controlling your emotions, or are they controlling you? Do you catch yourself spiraling off of something that hasn’t even happened yet? Living sovereign means sitting with your emotions without judgment. Noticing them, feeling them, but not letting them run your life.

Discernment also flows from the body. Your body will say yes or no before your mind can rationalize it. Trust that. The more you listen, the more you build a relationship with your inner knowing.

**2\. Honor the Divine Feminine Within**

Ladies, womb holders, goddesses of the diaspora you carry ancient codes. Codes of healing, nurturing, creation, intuition, multidimensional, and loving. Living sovereign means honoring ALL of her.

But so often, we’ve been forced into roles that push us away from our divine essence. As women, especially Black and Afro-Indigenous women, many of us had to be mother, father, nurturer, provider, and protector all at once. That survival energy is real but it’s not the fullness of who we are and doesn’t represent the capacity of power that we hold.

Returning to the divine feminine means reclaiming our softness, intuition, creativity, multidemensionality and sensuality. It means giving yourself permission to flow, to nurture yourself, and to lead from your inner goddess instead of society’s programming.

**3\. Commit to Your Truth**

To live sovereign, you gotta know what your truth is and then stand on it unapologetically. Say what you mean and mean it sacredly. That doesn’t mean being reckless or harsh it means being aligned with your soul, not living in lies, people-pleasing, or half-truths.

But here’s the thing: you can’t speak your truth if you don’t know it. That’s why coming back to your body, journaling, sitting in silence, and connecting with your ancestors is so important. It helps you peel back the programming and hear your true voice.

**4\. Spiritual Protection**

Sovereignty requires boundaries not just in the physical, but also in the spiritual. Protecting your energy, your home, and your spirit is sacred work. Whether it’s through prayer, ancestral connection, cleansing rituals, or simply saying “no,” spiritual protection ensures you remain grounded and sovereign in a world constantly trying to pull you off course.

**5\. Reconnect with Your Ancestors**

This journey is not meant to be walked alone. Your ancestors have walked before you, and they are guiding you. They hold the keys to your healing, your purpose, and your alignment. When you connect with them, you’re no longer living life blind you’re walking with a council of wisdom and love that’s rooting for you every step of the way.

**Final Word**

To live sovereign is to choose yourself. It’s to move with intention, not fear. It’s to honor your divine essence, set boundaries, and embody the truth of who you are. It doesn’t happen overnight it’s a journey of unlearning, remembering, and reclaiming.

But hear me clearly: you are worthy of sovereignty. You are worthy of living in alignment with your purpose, your DNA, and your soul. And as you embody sovereignty, you heal not just yourself but your lineage.

So ask yourself today: Am I living sovereign, or am I living programmed? The answer will guide you toward your next steps.
