From battered and tattered 
And Broken into pieces
To built up and strong
Despite all of the wrongs

Diminished and devalued
But my spirit is rescued

I wore shame and guilt 
Like the attire of a peasant
I forgot who I was
Restored to greatness into the present 

Like a phoenix, I rose
From the ashes

Beaten no more
I dance to a new drum
From the spirit of my ancestors 
Is where I come from

Strong and courageous, they were
And so am I

Victorious and mighty 
That’s who we are
Victims we could never be
For this is the bloodline of royalty

No matter what they said
Or what they claim

I will live my life to the fullest 
And I will gain, immortality

It’s only for a spell
So live the life well

Because tomorrow isn’t promised to anyone 
Better live this life right 

To the fullest of my potential 
Righting karmic cycles
Understanding more
With possibilities galore 

With every twist and turn
That teaches the lessons 
I’m grateful for every bit of it
Life is a gift and full of blessons
