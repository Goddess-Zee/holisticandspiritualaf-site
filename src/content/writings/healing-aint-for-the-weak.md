*Healing ain’t for the weak*.

I wish someone had told me that when I started the journey but it’s too late now, I’m too far in. I can remember my last family reunion. I was about 11 years old shortly after my beloved great-grandmother Nettie Brantley transitioned. She was the most stable thing I had to a family. Even though I was raised by my mother and in a house with my two siblings, I felt like I was alone in the world. I could still remember the unconditional love I felt for my great-grandmother. She was a very introspective soul who seemed to be guided by her inner realm all the time, like me. When I moved back to the States as a child, our time spent together was some of my best memories. My great-grandmother babysat me and it was the most peaceful moment of my early childhood. There was no rush, no sense of urgency to be anything or anywhere and every moment felt like a flow, like floating with the currents of a calm river. There weren’t any masks that needed to be worn just simple, authenticity, and acceptance of the present moment.

It’s almost like my little spirit needed that before the turbulent waters of life that lie ahead of me. I can still remember the telepathic relationship that we had. There were very few words between us but great understanding and she made the best lunches, ham and cheese sandwiches. I can still remember anticipating those soft batch cookies that she would heat up in the oven until the chocolate was melty.  I never complained about taking a nap and enjoyed waking up for the last few hours spent with her. After that, I was back in a world of noise, chaos, dysfunction, and misunderstanding.

In her elder years when she became bedridden, I would often escape the circus of noise and confusion when I went to my grandmother’s house to visit. My grandmother took care of my great-grandmother, my great-grandfather, and my grandfather and now my grandmother lives with my mother. I’ve never seen an elder family member of mine inside of a nursing home. When the family became loud and excited by each other‘s company and conversation, it seemed like no one was listening to understand but talking to be understood. I would often sneak away upstairs to my great-grandmother‘s room and find that peace and solitude, I remembered as a young child. During this time of her life, she slept most of the time and I would watch her TV in her room that had no remote control, for a while there wasn’t even cable, but it wasn’t so much the TV as much as her presence that I was seeking. We continued that telepathic relationship and she would wake up from her peaceful slumber and look over to see if I was still there. Our eyes would connect soul to soul and she would be back off to other dimensions in her earthly slumber. When she took her final breath of life on this earth, I was heartbroken. That was the first heartbreak I ever experienced in my life. I didn’t know anything about ancestral lineages, past lives, or purpose yet, but I knew that a piece of her would always remain with me in my heart ♥️ and so she did.

Fast-forward and Life went on the days, got further and further away from our physical connection in my life, and my understanding began. For a long time, I felt like I didn’t have any connection with anyone that closely on the planet, and it was lonely at times.

I time travel to growing up without that strong matriarchal presence in my life, I encountered many twists and turns in the valley of what seemed like the shadow of death. I now know today that my specific spiritual path called for an uneasy beginning for a fuller more rich and purposeful later life and every turn was necessary for my spiritual growth and evolution process. It seemed like I was in high school and I blinked and I was already a mother to someone else. I was ill-prepared, to say the least, I didn’t have the most exemplary childhood of what a loving, nurturing, and supportive home looked like so how was I supposed to be able to give that to someone else? I blinked again and became a mother of two children. That’s when the shit really hit the fan. If I didn’t know what to do with one, I damn sure was clueless about a second.

The responsibility of raising two little spirits that were gifted to me temporarily because ultimately they are God‘s children. As parents, we only get a special allotted amount of time to do the best we can to shape and mold these little spirits returned on their life’s path. Talk about important work, my young mind, barely out of being a teenager couldn’t even fathom a concept like that. Let alone that we choose our parents to fulfill our life’s purpose. That really grew me up when I discovered the idea that I chose my Mother and Father but I’m grateful for their part in my journey today.

So I went on to make mistakes and get things right, and through it all I grew up with my kids. I had times when things were good but I would be dishonest if I didn’t admit that some of the behaviors that I grew up with didn’t bleed into their childhood. I was a young mom who lacked the maturity to handle things like a responsible adult all of the time and sometimes I didn’t know any better. The one thing I had going for me was that I was able to tap into mental health and spiritual development while they were still young. I realized that certain patterns had to be healed for me to break unhealthy generational cycles. I was constantly seeking a better way to deal with my emotions and always taking my girls along for the ride so that they could experience different forms of spirituality and healing. Even though sometimes it seems like they could care less about any of it, I know that the seed has been planted and that they will take what they need and leave the rest when the time is right. 

I was always transparent with my girls about the importance of healing generational trauma to be able to make a better tomorrow for our lineage moving forward. Now that my girls are young adults some of my mistakes before I knew better have come up for them and it’s been difficult to hear at times. Hell, it’s been downright painful. Knowing better and being a more mature healed version of myself,  has allowed me to hold space for them as they communicate how they experienced feelings from the past and the healing continues. I have taken responsibility for the error of my ways and I work daily to mend these wounds that my children suffer. This was never allowed for me even after I became an adult. I realize that I was dealing with unhealed people who were doing the best that they could and some of them refused to ever take responsibility for their actions because they couldn’t see the error in their ways and that’s okay. I realize that it wasn’t part of their journey, but for me, I have the responsibility to heal myself first, help my children to heal, and ensure that the next generation doesn’t have to experience the same trauma that we did.

This is how we break cycles and this is how we heal generations and lineages. I am aware of this and since I have healed the majority of my issues through a combination of therapy and self-healing techniques and rituals I’m now experiencing the effects of trauma passed on to my children and we are healing from that too. Real healing isn’t all glamorous at every stage of healing, sometimes it looks like anger, lashing out, intense feelings, remembering, and tears before it gets better. Sometimes healing hurts first and in my experience, it’s better to deal with those feelings rather than avoid them. If I avoid those feelings, then the healing stops I know that the universe never gives me more than I can handle, and as I heal and elevate, so does my lineage. When I heal, my children heal and their children heal, and the cycle of healing continues. I’m grateful for the time that I was able to experience the unconditional love of my great-grandmother and all of the others along my journey that showed me a better way of living.

Zipporah Banyay

9-12-2024
