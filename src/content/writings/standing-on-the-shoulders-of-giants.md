Imagine journeying through galaxies landing on planet Earth, experiencing a multitude of lifetimes, gathering and gaining infinite wisdom along the way, and still not knowing how magnificent you are.

*This is my story…*

For years, I navigated this planet. Not having a true sense of self like so many others, I was like a chameleon, trying to find some sense of comfort or ease in belonging and human kindness. As a child, I always wanted to fit in and to be normal. I wanted to be seen, heard, accepted, and valued. *Isn’t that what we all want?* I was a quiet child, very internal but I was also imaginative and creative. If only I could see the magnificence of me and those characteristics at that age. Unfortunately, I didn’t have anyone pouring into me and telling me how smart and beautiful I was, what a great person I was, and that I could be anything and anybody I wanted.

*But was that really a negative thing*? Not necessarily, I speak a lot about healing generational trauma that is stored in our DNA, this comes from childhood and how we experienced life coming into our own and even past lifetimes. Having healed a lot of that trauma and making it to the other side and into purpose I realize that every experience shaped me into today’s version of me and today I know my ancestors were guiding me for as long as I could remember.

My healing journey started 20 years ago, being thrust into facilities that allowed my Mind, Body & Spirit the necessary break to have conversations about healing. Topics like meditation, the power of the breath, the Mind, Body connection, and the inner child were the themes. These were like foreign languages to me at the time and just like any foreign language, it took time to develop and understand. Like the syntaxes of a new language, I had to learn to hear the voices of my ancestors, spirit guides, and intuition. Similar to learning the everyday use of a new language versus the proper grammar that we are usually exposed to when first learn a new language, I had to learn to break down the codes and decipher the difference between my higher self and my lower self. There were lots of frustrations and in the beginning, my lower self won a lot more than my Higher self, and all the same, I received the messages and lessons that were meant for me in ways that only I could understand.

The vastness of consciousness is inconceivable to the human mind, but to the spirit self and eventually tapping into the soul, we get a glimpse of what eternal existence looks and feels like. My journey navigated me to some really cool spaces. I would go through these patterns of learning, understanding, implementation and eventually helping others to navigate the complexities of having a spiritual awakening. I had teachers and mentors along the way, but never for too long. The most consistent teacher in my life was my experience being guided by my ancestors.

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/00670e36-14ff-4196-b9cc-4d2be367d2de_1280x1280.png)

As I continued my journey, I wanted to learn as much as I could about myself and where I came from I started tapping into some amazing facts about my bloodline ancestral lineage. Not just the stories of where they came from places such as Africa, the Caribbean, and on my mother‘s side all of my ancestors are native and indigenous to the land we now call America and were people we describe as black today. We were already here before the African transatlantic slave trade and there were no such descriptions as black or white during this time. We were known by our tribes and family lineage. *That’s right, not all of us so-called black peoples’ ancestors were brought over here on slave ships from Africa.*

Some of us have indigenous bloodlines that are native to the land we were born to. Now, for some, this statement may be tapping the confusion synapses in the brain because of all of the misinformation, and the trouble that was gone through to destroy records. Indigenous people were already here long before Europeans began making their voyages to America. *Who were those people and what of their offspring?* As I always say, do your own research, don’t take my word for it! Thanks to the age of information and the internet information is available for all to see but some of us choose to see only what we want to see if it supports a paradigm that we are comfortable existing in.

For a long time as a little kid, I only knew about the particular tribes that made treaties with the United States, and those Native Americans were depicted to be only of one type, meaning they had a specific and distinct physical characterization that was different than mine. The story that was sold to me was that I came from slaves, and there was never any conversation about who my ancestors were before slavery. Quite naturally hearing this, and seeing images and depictions of people who look like you being in a state of submission, scarcity, inferiority and fear would be enough to make not only the little black girl feel less about herself, but also allow the justification of other people to treat certain people who looked like me in all sorts of evil ways.

Over time a person doesn’t think very much about themselves or even care to hear another bad luck story about their ancestors. Some of them long for this sense of connection with their ancestors and begin to reach beyond the bloodline to star seeds and light beings, all of these other beings are also very valid and important, but it’s sort of like skipping steps to reach beyond the magnificence that’s already there. I won’t get too much into the cosmic family because that’s a completely different writing and we’ve already gone down the rabbit hole a little bit here with this story. When skipping steps, we can then begin to miss the path meant for us along our journey and start to take detours into information only lanes. The good news about this is against our better judgment our ancestors are still guiding and protecting us. It’s just much easier when we have a conscious connection with them for us to begin to elevate faster.

Navigating back to finding out about myself, I remember in 2016 asking my grandmother about our ancestors and didn’t seem to get much information from her at the time and I felt defeated by an insurmountable task. I can recall the well of tears in my eyes and the volcanic eruption of anger from deep inside of myself because I felt like nobody cared but I didn’t let that hold me back, my ancestors knew that I was trying to make conscious contact. What I didn’t realize is that when I asked my grandmother and mother, I was actually activating things inside of them that would begin a beautiful process of remembrance of their ancestors as well. Over the years, my grandmother would sit and recall stories of her siblings, her parents, grandparents, and cousins, and I was fascinated. I still am today, I feel blessed to be able to get these stories straight from an ancestor returned and the database she holds in her DNA. My grandmother is 91 years old, and I’m so grateful to have her.

The stories she spoke of were not about people of a weak disposition at all. Their stories were riddled with personality, humor, strength, courage, and wisdom. I began to find the similarities and some of my personality traits and find the commonality in my ancestors and why I’m on the path I’m on today. I had already begun to build a relationship but now I had names to add to my venerations. I also learned a tremendous amount about my community ancestors. These would be ancestors not related by blood, but through our kindred strength and ability to overcome oppression in a culture that they lived in and I live in today. This includes civil rights activists, inventors, business moguls, and spiritual teachers and the list and supply is endless if we seek it. I learned about the major contributions they made to civilization through inventions and intellectual properties most of us still use today. I started learning about my story and not *his-story*. I began to learn the truth, and the magnificence that lay in my DNA all along, and I have the awareness that at any time I can call on these knowledgeable ancient ones, some more ancient than the others, to assist me in my daily living. Understanding this about myself, changed the trajectory of how I felt about myself. I am them and they are me, as I rise, they rise in the astral plane and other dimensions. Knowing this, I could no longer go back to a storyline of self-pity, but I move through the Earth, feeling empowered, and knowing that I stand on the shoulders of Giants.

If you’re someone looking to connect with your ancestors, I encourage you to start with the elders in your family. Anyone older than you can provide the information you need to get you started and that can lead to an amazing experience. Don’t be discouraged by the lack of information that you get in the beginning, you may be the first in your family to wake up the generations before and after you, and gaining this understanding about yourself and your ancestry is a priceless discovery.

Zipporah Banyay
