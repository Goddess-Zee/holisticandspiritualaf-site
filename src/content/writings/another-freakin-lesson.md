It was 4 AM and I burst up from my restless slumber of tossing and turning from worry. It had been about a good two weeks and the responsibilities, and worries had finally caught up to me. I was battling being dissatisfied at a particular job, not the profession, but the actual job was *really* getting under my skin. I 100% understand that a job can be something that just pays the bills. However, if the stressors from that job are due to poor management and lack of employee benefits, and doesn’t support good mental health, that's a problem for me and it has to be replaced. That’s what’s been going on at this gig, I was long overdue a departure months ago. I just got too complacent…

I started feeling the need for a change about six months ago, not only was being stressed beyond belief, but I literally felt like I was being picked on because I was one of the BEST despite it all and I was constantly trying to offer suggestions for improvement to management which typically doesn’t go over well when management is not good at what they do. I can’t help myself, that's how I show up everywhere, doing my best. My friends have told me that the problem was, I was doing too much at that job and I should've given them about 50% of me but I don’t know how to be like that, I only know how to change things that no longer serve me. Stress, emotional abuse, and add that to being underpaid for my skill set and we’re on the borderline of a walkout any given day.

As much as I’d love to do that, in the adult world and land of responsibilities that comes in the form of digital notifications in the amount of three and four figures that doesn’t exactly work without preparation and planning. Couple that with navigating relationships that have made it past their eviction notice and are now living rent free in my head, and you’re dealing with someone already on the spectrum of losing it or at the very least acting out of character. Roll that into a very close loved one who won’t take practical suggestions that can help shift the course of their life and you’ve got yourself a big fat burrito of overwhelm and worry. 

One of the hardest things about being an empath, intuitive, and facilitator of healing is being able to see very clearly in another what needs to be healed and changed but at times lacking that same discernment in my own life.

*Out of the darkness comes the light.* 

*From chaos comes order.* 

That’s what I’ve learned but in the midst of it it just seems like chaotic darkness and I can’t see *shit*. Absolutely zero clarity because now my emotions are running the show. I find myself throwing the tarot cards like a newbie, expecting a different answer and getting frustrated with my ancestors, *lack of communication.* I’ve been here before and every time I’m either missing the lesson or I have to wait for more to be revealed and my ancestors become eerily quiet. I’m no stranger to this and I know the rules. Some things I’m absolutely powerless over, some things need to be accepted, and some things I need to change. Trying to put these three in perspective and place them in the correct order can sometimes be a challenge if I’m hell-bent on getting my way.

For at least a year now, I’ve managed to maintain my morning ritual of getting up and starting my day with God, the ancestors, meditation, and a big cup of coffee, I haven’t quite made it back to my tea rituals yet. The last two weeks I had a guest from out of town and it really threw me off my game while I enjoyed the company, my routines were misplaced, and I found myself not making that strong spirit connection in the mornings. Could this have something to do with my state of mind? *Possibly, Absolutely.*

Either way, I found myself at a crossroads and I could no longer continue feeling like this. I tried to pray and realized that my prayers weren’t coming out, I would begin to pray, but then something would stop me before I could actually say it. I realized that I needed to call in the Great Spirit of the universe and everything in it because things were out of my control: the relationship that was over despite my best efforts, The adult that didn’t want to take my advice, even though it’s sound and practical, the constant rejections after many job application submissions and job interviews. These things were all obvious signs that this was bigger than me and I had to call the spirit of the universe to bring in divine wisdom. That’s when I realized it was time to *let go and let God*. I was out of options and what I was doing wasn’t working, I found myself able to pray again, but my prayers didn’t come out as demands. I immediately got back on my morning rituals because I definitely felt the lack of support from the universe without that important practice and the most interesting thing started to happen. I started getting clarity.

I started hearing other people say exactly what I needed to hear, I started reading short excerpts that seemed tailored to my situation. It’s as if great spirit was speaking to me through these people. My ancestors got vocal again, the cards were the same because the message never changed, but I did. I was able to see how when life is a struggle and I’m going against the natural flow and rhythm of it  I’m at dis-ease of the mind and body. When I was a massage therapist, I remember telling people that all disease starts in the mind and manifest to the physical body if left unchecked. 

Stress, frustration, and worry are my energetic signals manifesting physically to alert me that I’m disconnected and out of alignment. At the beginning of every podcast episode, I often say “I hope everyone is taking care of themselves Mind Body & Spirit because if either one of those is out of balance, then we are out of alignment.” Being an intuitive doesn’t mean that I have all the answers all the time I still have my lessons to grow through, but if I don’t stay connected to source energy, then I receive limited energy and that’s when clarity goes out the window.

For me, the lesson was, that I was trying to control, and steer the ship of my life when the universe, God, and my ancestors were protecting me from things that no longer support the next phase of the journey. I am grateful for these painful experiences and I’m even more grateful for my energy body and my spirit body having the awareness to recognize when things are off. I’m still in the midst of the changes, but I’m less in fear because I know that God, my ancestors, and the universe support my highest good even if I don’t know exactly what that is right now. There’s so much power in the simple phrase *let go and let God*.

Zipporah Banyay

8-3-2024

Update: As I wrote the last phrase *let go and let God*, I received a phone call that completely changed the trajectory of what I was experiencing with the adult not taking suggestions, that adult is my youngest daughter and she decided to listen to me and come back home and that was a load of worry released. Something tragic happened with another family member that helped her to see what I was saying and she had a change of heart. This is evidence that sometimes I don’t know what the universe has planned and that sometimes letting go is the best thing that can be done for the best outcome.
