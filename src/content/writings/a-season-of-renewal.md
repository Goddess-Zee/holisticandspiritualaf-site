Sitting here in a tiny but quaint studio apartment, and I couldn’t be happier. The constant purring and cuddles next to me let me know that my cat welcomes the change, too! This cat is the most well-traveled cat I’ve ever had, and for 13 years, we’ve been on many new adventures. At this point, she just walks right into her carrier. I chuckle at the thought of that because this reminds me of her younger years and my daughters and I trying to get her sassy self in the cat carrier, I *literally* had to turn the thing standing up and drop her in because she would be hanging on by her toes to avoid going into the carrier. Lol. They say pets carry their owner’s personality, and she definitely embodies my feisty side. My current living situation is temporary until my permanent residence is ready in a few weeks, and honestly, I’m grateful for what seemed like challenges in the beginning. Everything happened in Divine in order and direction to help me get to a new place of alignment. It’s funny how shit works out sometimes ya dig.

Our lease at the old place was up at the end of February, literally and figuratively, so I knew the change was coming. Our ending came months before, and we were just existing in a business transaction. This move and transition meant the ending of a hurricane of a relationship that set out to destroy me but actually ended up, teaching me more about myself. I learned what I was willing to put up with and what I wouldn’t, which isn’t very much with the fiery spirit that I carry. I learned why I choose certain partners in my life, which brought a deep level of healing. Old inner child wounds crashed over me like the waves of an ocean during a storm surge. Old news became current, and I was faced with a huge shadow of myself!

When relationships don’t go well, it’s easy to blame the other person, but a lesson is never to focus on the other person as much as it is to see what patterns I’m reliving. So that’s what I did, and over time, I was pleasantly surprised to realize that these patterns of behavior were blocking me from reaching higher levels of consciousness! These wounds were so deep that I didn’t even recognize they were there. I had been living with them so long, they seemed a part of me, maybe more than a lifetime or two.

![Image by José Augusto Camargo from Pixabay](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/a5840ddf-fd69-43ed-8c0d-4cf3ca097e64_640x1280.png)

Out of every lesson always comes a blessing for me, even if I can’t see it at first. This one had instant results, mainly on my energy body. I never realized how much weight I was carrying. My ancestors had warned me against this relationship, and quite defiantly, me being the human having a spiritual experience that I am, I did it anyway. I took a detour, the shortcut that ended up taking me longer to get to this place of transformation. I recently had a friend tell me to stop collecting red flags. I felt that one in the gut because I had to have at least about 100 of ‘em’ within three months, and no, my intuition wasn’t broke either. My ego was just hell-bent on proving them all wrong. That being said, I’m still grateful for the opportunity to grow and that it wasn’t too crazy of a relationship, just a mismatch. I’m also grateful for being able to see things through healed lenses, and that really changes my perspective of everything. There was no need to have a crazy and dramatic blow up of a break up like my unhealed self experienced in the past, and typically my part was as equally bad as the other person. I simply got my shit, said my goodbyes and blocked that ass on every channel of communication there is! No need to leave any cords connected or soul attachments, *right*?

Today, I get to recognize and acknowledge that every experience is happening for my benefit and that the universe is always conspiring for my growth and manifestation and not the other way around. When I’m coasting along and things are all well from time to time, I need an experience like this to help me go deeper into self discovery and transformation. At the end of the day, I’m removing blockages that help to expand my consciousness and get closer to my true self. Just in time for spring renewal and a new year. Throughout our separation, I had a lot of good seeds being planted as well. I left a toxic job for another that is better aligned with my spiritual maintenance and growth. I wrote the second e-book, [The Ancestral Altar Guide: How to Create and Use an Altar to Connect with Your Ancestors](https://www.amazon.com/Ancestral-Altar-Guide-Create-Ancestors-ebook/dp/B0DV46KQZ5?ref_=ast_author_mpb), I incorporated live guided meditations on TikTok and YouTube. I added guided meditations to [Insight Timer](https://insighttimer.com/godesszee), an app that I use frequently for my personal meditations. I created more classes for the collective consciousness, and I made more soul connections and helped others to grow along their spiritual journey. Throughout it all, I kept planting seeds and staying rooted in purpose. It’s been a true time of renewal, and I look forward to living the new version of myself as I continue to grow in self-discovery.

Happy Spring!
