Hey, beautiful souls. It’s Goddess Zee and today, I have another message straight from Spirit. So, take a deep breath, get comfortable, and prepare to receive.

I’ll give a little context behind this for clarity …

There was a time when my mind never shut up. Constant overthinking, worry, and doubt. That endless cycle of anxiety had me questioning if I was even hearing my intuition or just my fears speaking louder than spirit. I knew I was being called to something deeper, but I didn’t know how to get there. I was headed for self-destruction living life in the fast lane going about 105 mph. I was a *good* girl in a world of *REAL* badasses. I met all types of monsters on the journey, I was like Nemo trying to find my way home to self and my encounters with the people of the world only exacerbated my inner confusion.

And then, meditation found me…

![Image by Tiểu Minh from Pixabay](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/8e8fd9f4-865d-45b0-b571-9b0d2aad08cd_700x426.jpeg)

At first, I thought, *How’s this gonna work, I’m just sitting here doing nothing and listening to soft music!* But that wasn’t the point. The point was to sit and just **Be t**o listen and to feel. To distinguish the voice of my ancestors, my guides, and my higher self from the noise of my ego. past, conditioning, and everything else.

I started with five minutes. Just five and let me tell you that five minutes turned into clarity. Into peace and eventually into power. Soon after, I was **tapping into the divine messages that were waiting for me all along**.

I was hooked after that and I remember getting up to 45 mins a session and something amazing started to happen. I was leaving this physical plane and tapping into another realm of existence. That’s all it took to pique this seeker’s curiosity. I had finally found something that helped me to feel better from the inside out and to get the hell out of the ghetto existence I had created for myself with my thoughts. I was able to learn about myself and this helped me survive the world around me and begin to thrive. Now don’t get me wrong, it took some other work along the way but meditation was definitely the foundation for all of my healing and spiritual development. That was 20 years ago and the journey has taken me to some amazing places.

Now on to some current news! Goddess Zee is officially **certified as an accredited meditation facilitator** (yes, leveling up over here!), and I’m hosting **live guided meditations on TikTok and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@holisticandspiritualafpodcast)** so that YOU can experience the same transformation. It’s amazing how everything comes back around a full 360 degrees before entering a new quantum shift.

Back to the message from spirit: If you’re starting, or are on your spiritual journey (*which is everyone alive right now)* meditation is pretty much non-negotiable for growth. No pressure, you don’t have to start out with meditating for one hour plus, as a matter of fact, you don’t have to meditate at all but if you want to change your life and quiet the noise in your mind to find inner peace meditation is a great place to start. Meditation is a practice and it takes time to be developed and to blossom into something esoteric. Give yourself time and be gentle with yourself as you grow.

![Image by Daniel Calabrese from Pixabay](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/7d70289f-d8a7-46b6-82ad-c726c5ae93f7_700x466.jpeg)

**Why Meditation is a Non-Negotiable for Your Spiritual Journey**

-   It quiets the mind so you can actually hear divine guidance.
    
-   It strengthens your connection with your ancestors and higher self.
    
-   It helps you break free from generational patterns and heal from the inside out.
    
-   And most importantly — it reminds you that *you are already whole, already worthy, and already powerful.*
    

I want to personally invite you to join me in one of my live sessions. Whether you stay for five minutes or the whole experience, just start. You don’t have to be “good” at meditation. You don’t have to have all the answers. You just have to **show up for yourself**.

So, are you ready?
