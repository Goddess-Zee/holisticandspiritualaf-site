Mother’s Day is so much more than a commercial holiday in my eyes. It’s reverence to the sacred portal that brings forth life into this earthly dimension, a moment to reflect on the Divine Feminine energy that births us all, sustains us, and carries us through this human experience. It’s also a day to acknowledge the living, breathing women who held that sacred role for us in this lifetime, even if that role was complicated or painful. For me, that woman is my mother.

My mother is one of the strongest and most courageous women I’ve ever known. I watched her raise three children as a single mother, pushing through poverty, state assistance, and the harsh realities that often come with both. I witnessed her go from working 3 jobs, to working in the car factory doing the back-breaking work, and eventually making it to management. Her relentless grind meant moving us from barely surviving to a life where we could thrive in the upper middle class. That’s divine magic in action. Everything about our lives changed, the neighborhoods we lived in, the schools we went to, and of course, the types of experiences we had. All of this afforded us choices and opportunities that she never had as a child.

She made the ultimate sacrifice! I’ll be honest, there were times when I couldn’t see it, times when the hurt and the trauma spoke louder than the love. It took my own journey of healing, shadow work, and raising my own daughters to truly *see* her, to accept her decisions, and to respect the powerful, complex, and deeply loving mother she was and still is.

We often speak about the generational trauma that’s passed down, but it’s equally important to name the *gifts* our ancestors and mothers pass down, too. Once I could shift my perspective and see both sides of the spectrum, I realized that my mother had not only passed down resilience but also wisdom, devotion, tenacity, and a deep reverence for education and knowledge.

One of the greatest gifts my mother ever gave me was teaching me to read before I even entered school. That simple, intentional act sparked a lifetime of learning, curiosity, and connection to something greater than myself. Books became my first portals to freedom, and knowledge became my sword and shield. That thirst for knowledge led me to heal deeper and eventually gain knowledge of self. That early gift planted a seed that would one day grow into a deep well of wisdom I could pass on to my daughters.

And now, I’m a mother. I have my own little lineage two powerful daughters, and a beautiful grandbaby. And as I mother them, I feel my mother in me. I feel *the Divine Mother* I am becoming, the divine essence, the infinite love, and the lineage of women who held us all, whether we knew it or not.

***To my mother:***

***I respect you more than you’ll ever know. Your sacrifices were not in vain. Your prayers were not unanswered. Your journey lives on in me, in my daughters, and in every life I touch.***

This Mother’s Day, I honor the Divine Mother in all her forms the fierce protectress, the nurturer, the teacher, the shadow-walker, the healer. Mother Earth and the many souls she nurtures. I honor every mother doing her best to break cycles while holding it all together. I honor you.

**And I honor the Mother within me.**
