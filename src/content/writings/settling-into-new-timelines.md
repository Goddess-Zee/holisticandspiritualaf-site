Writing is like a soothing jacuzzi after an intense leg day in the gym. I’ve got to tell you I’m really loving this writing journey. For me, it aligns with my spirit and soul and what I’m feeling on the inside. I can’t just write to write. I have to feel something that ignites the passion and fire inside of me and compels me to wanna share my inner thoughts and downloads. It’s been a while since I’ve touched pen to paper, life has been busy, and life has been full as I began making the ascent into higher consciousness.

I love that for me, it’s been a journey, but now I am settling into alignment, as much of the planet is right now. I’m in my new space, and the address is a number 5! Numerologically, which stands for alignment. When I tell you everything about this place is perfect for *ME*, it’s like the universe cherry-picked it at the perfect harvest time specifically for me. Fully renovated, spacious, great location, great neighbors, plenty of sunlight, and most importantly, peaceful. It’s like the ultimate calm that matches everything else in my life, and I’m starting to experience something else called joy. The exuberance for life and happiness for just *being*. This tells me that the healing was worth it, the dark nooks and crannies of the shadow soul that allowed me to embrace the light, and now share that with other people.

There have been so many changes within me, and I often find myself unconsciously trying to hang on and salvage what was. My ancestors and spirit guides nudged me to open my eyes to the possibilities of everything my heart desires. I remember when this sounded like woo woo, and now I’m living it! I finally realigned the frequency in my DNA to be able to live the life of my dreams.

I came across a video where a lady spoke of how rich she was, and people told her that she wasn’t rich because she didn’t have a lot of money or a big house and fancy cars. She turned to explain to them that she was rich because of the happiness, joy, and peace she has in her life. That’s how I feel today, *RICH* and abundance is a frequency! I remember there was a time when I felt like my happiness was dependent upon the things on the outside and today I know more than ever that I’ve always been blessed and covered, but I wasn’t always happy, peaceful, and damn sure not joyful like I’ve been experiencing recently. I have great relationships of all kinds, I smile and laugh from the bottom of my soul. I remember when I cried from that same place, but every tear was worth it. I experience food like it’s the first time, and the love that I have in my life, originating from myself, is magical. I could go on, but you get the point.

*This is where it gets really good!*

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/dc4f9546-22c8-4113-8d4a-b5515f3f4a07_700x700.png)

Last month was my birthday/Earthborn, #AriesNation, another trip around the sun, and I can feel the timelines aligning as I am no longer acknowledging the Gregorian calendar nor the timelines of the previous world order.

*Let the order of nature and the divine feminine rise.*

My last writing took place at a time when I was at a pause in the transit, a lot of that had to do with retrogrades and Neptune entering into Aries, so things on the physical we’re moving slowly, but internally very rapidly. Some of the transformation for me has been about balance, learning to give myself the care and attention that I give to others. Recognizing when it’s time to be alone and being comfortable with that. Extremely heightened intuition, constant evolving of my spiritual gifts, including mediumship and channeling, which is always been there. *Duh!* It’s as if the veil has been lifted, and I am embracing my true self for the first time. All of Me!

I know with all the transits that we’ve been experiencing over the last few years and now coming at a rapid pace has been preparing me for this final transition out of the old and into what is always been.

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/43622db9-74b1-41ea-9031-36640adcce84_700x700.png)

These kinds of transitions, moving from one timeline to another, aligning with the natural order and essence of my spirit and soul’s assignment, gives me peace and clarity. I’m connecting with other healers, spiritualists, star seeds, and chosen ones, and we’re moving into formation for the important work for the evolution of human consciousness. What a magnificent time to be on the planet right here and right now, and with everything that’s going on, I have absolutely zero fear and total trust in the divine alignment happening at this time. I’m loving seeing all the people tapping into the woo woo shit that they called me crazy for, but I never let go of. but that’s not the point, the point is we’re moving into new dimensions, and either you’re moving in alignment or you’re hanging onto the old reality whichever you choose, you will experience. One might ask, how are you at peace, happy and joyful with all of the shit that’s happening around the planet and it’s because I’m experiencing a different reality and I recognize that that 3-D physical material realm is an illusion and always has been.

We are experiencing times when you can have 100 people in a room and all of them be on different frequencies and dimensions of reality. So, which one will you choose? It’s very simple to see where you are now. Ask yourself a few questions.

1.  Am I struggling to let go of outdated habits and patterns?
    
2.  Do I feel a pull towards understanding more about my purpose?
    
3.  Am I feeling disconnected to my things that I’ve always done?
    
4.  Am I becoming more attuned to higher vibratory conversations and content?
    

These questions will gauge whether you’re having a transformation and what frequencies you’re aligning to. These astrological transits aren’t waiting on anybody, and this Earth is literally splitting into two dimensions, and it’s either grow or get left! Which will you choose? Do you continue to ignore the signs and messages and let go, or do you walk through the portal that the universe has waiting for you?

The choice is always yours!
