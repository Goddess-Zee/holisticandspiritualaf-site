I’ve been meditating on using energy wisely for the past couple of days because it applies to my life today. Last night I did a reading because things are moving fast and in alignment after some much-needed downtime and for a lot longer time than I expected.

I started working on myself through shadow work and increasing prayers and meditation. My vibration also raised and quite naturally people started to become, attracted to me, as well as negative entities repelled by me!

So I go off on this journey and start helping people not understanding what’s happening behind the scenes, but feeling compelled to do it anyway.

Throughout the whole time, I’m praying meditating, doing readings, etc.… and I start getting more clarity into my purpose, which is helping other people to reconnect with their divine selves.

Now, you can’t possibly imagine how exciting that is and how full circle that is for me. I started my journey 18 years ago and knew deep inside myself that I was supposed to be doing this type of work and now we’re finally here. I was helping people along the way, but I had a tremendous amount of healing to do myself so I wasn’t at my fullest potential sometimes.

I remember wanting to help people who didn’t want to help themselves, seeing something in people who couldn’t see it in themselves, offering holistic services to people who weren’t serious about healing, etc.... and wasting a lot of my energy. Sometimes I was doing these things and not replenishing my energy bank, which led me physical energy drain and dissatisfaction.

Recently my worldly responsibilities have increased, and I see the need for balance. This brings me to not burning energy foolishly. As I step into this new phase of heightened intuition, spiritual gifts, and abilities. I feel the need to focus all of my energy so that it’s at its maximum use and potential.

I recently did a class assignment where I got a chance to analyze my life and the new responsibilities, and I was able to see where there was an imbalance in my time. It’s important to understand that time is also energy,(literally) so I can’t be Burnin that up either with foolish activities, conversations, people, or anything that’s not aligned with the new direction.

I noticed that I started feeling drained like I used to feel sometimes after leading a Reiki circle. Now that’s common because you’re working on several people, which can pull a lot of energy from one practitioner. However, if I’m having phone conversations with people and I’m feeling drained afterward, there’s clearly something wrong with that. If I am going to certain places and feeling drained, there’s something wrong with that. What a lot of people don’t understand about energy is energy is also an exchange, meaning that if I am giving, I should also be receiving. For example, in a paid course I get information for money, and I pay to get a pedicure for the care and aesthetics of my feet 👣. So why not the same in relationships, conversations, experiences, etc.?

That’s when I realized I was wasting valuable energy with people who either can’t or won’t reciprocate. That’s a NO for me. Taking care of myself means taking care of my energy body too and I will eradicate all time and energy wasters from this moment moving forward. Now, to do this requires a strong mind because I can’t be concerned about what others will think when they’re no longer able to waste my energy. I need to do what’s necessary for me mind, body, and spirit unapologetically. If I’m moving in alignment with purpose who could say anything against that? But trust they will and that’s okay, they’re no longer my concern. 

Now, back to the reading yesterday. I pulled the cards because I wanted clarity. There was still some apprehension about leaving some folks behind who would not be able to understand my moves. I wanted clarity on whether moving in this new direction to help people on a higher level would yield fruit.

It's my time, baby!!! As long as I stay in alignment and use the energy wisely I am aimed for success in this new journey endeavor of helping people. The universe, ancestors, and most importantly my creator wants this for me. "This is purpose-driven, ya'll" Now the lover's card had me like "Huh, wait what" But the lover's card can be about partnerships and relationships of all kinds and this lets me know that I will be building trusted relationships with people that are harmonious and helpful. The fool card lets me know that I'm headed on a new journey and phase of my life, this doesn't mean that I'll get all of the answers upfront but I always have the guidance of my creator, intuition, and ancestors guiding me along the way like they always have. This journey will have twists and turns just like they all do but I'm up for the challenge and I will not be wasting my energy where it doesn't belong.
