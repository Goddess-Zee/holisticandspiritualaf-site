Today’s writing was inspired by my comment on someone’s post. The person was talking about how proud they were of themselves, what a great parent they are, and how their parent was abusive to them and they were better than their parents. Sounds really familiar and I know this rhetoric all too well. For years I blamed my parents, I couldn’t stand my mama, didn’t wanna fuck with my daddy because he chose to raise his stepkids over me, etc… Baby, I was angry and my anger was justified and out of that same mouth I would turn around and tell you how much better of a mother I was to my girls despite the healing work that I had to do. I would say things like I’ll never be like my mother or even worse, wait till he gets older I’m not gonna be there for him. I was still hurt, I was still wounded and I was still deeply affected by the trauma that I had experienced in my life and that I blamed them for. For a long time, I wasn’t ready to heal that because I found some comfort in being the victim, telling the story, and keeping the blazing flame of anger hot and I got absolutely no satisfaction from this and that level of existence was killing me spiritually. Those statements and belief systems that I held were actually keeping me sick.

For those of you who are familiar with me. You know I’m an ancestor girly. I love my ancestors, and I always have walked with my ancestors since I was a child, they’ve guided me through many things in my life. My ancestors never judged this type of thinking they knew that I was still healing, and almost 3 years ago I did the deepest healing I’ve ever done in my life. It was the ultimate shadow work, the grand finale of the festering roots that kept reoccurring in my life causing havoc and disconnection to source energy. I went so deep in the shadows, determined to rid myself of anything that stood in the way of my full expression of God/Goddess expressing *in and as and through me.* I could have never imagined the journey that I was about to embark on.

We’ve all heard the saying from our spiritual teachers and mentors that we ***choose*** our parents, and we ***choose*** our path. We ***choose*** the parents that will assist us and fulfilling our life purpose, ”Bullshit” I would say! I would never choose her. But the ultimate reality is that those parents gave me a life that allowed me to walk in purpose to become a seeker, to heal generational trauma, and to break cycles. Their spirits made a contract for me to be the chosen one to heal the trauma of our lineage in the past generations and the future generations, and they did it well. They were under the covering of the old awning of generational trauma. They passed the torch to me to restore order for the new generation in our bloodline lineage. If they had behaved even the slightest bit differently, I wouldn’t be exactly who I am today and for that, I have mad respect for those people and their part in my journey. 

Back to being an ancestor girly always and forever. How in the hell could I pay reverence to my ancestors from 100 years ago, ascended masters, and the ancient ones, and not pay reverence to the portal that gave me life in this dimension Ase Oooo! *Come on now*, I feel like I’m having metaphysical church up in here this morning, I know some people ain’t gonna want to hear this because they are wherever they are on their journey and in their healing, and that’s okay I get it, I was there for a long time and I was in bondage too.  My experience has been that when I was able to heal on such a deep level, I had that understanding and compassion for my mother and father that I wrote about in my book **[The 5 Stages of Shadow Work](https://www.amazon.com/Stages-Shadow-Work-Zipporah-Banyay/dp/B0D57DR7GX/ref=tmm_pap_swatch_0?_encoding=UTF8&qid=&sr=)**. I was able to forgive them and experience another level of freedom. Forgiveness is a very very powerful form of healing that set me free from the shackles of trauma and elevates my ancestors of hundreds of thousands of years of trauma.

How exactly did it do that? 

![](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/5287f6fc-ff6f-45d8-9830-d8fad9bc4d40_1120x638.png)

As people of the diaspora and indigenous to the earth, warring with our parents is not natural to us. It has become a new norm from a world order that was designed to separate us from our cultures, communities, and spirituality and destroy our families. Before this, we were a community, we respected our elders. We helped one another. We loved and cared for each other. We shared everything, we experienced life through nature and the village raised the children. Whew, chile, that sent chills through my bones, just thinking about it! When I accepted my parents as they are, functioning under this paradigm of existence opposite from the natural order of life, when I forgave them for being just who they are, I was able to move forward, to the beginning of forming a healed relationship with them and a new story for the lineage to embark on. *”Ubuntu”*  I am because they were and that is how this ancestral connection thing works. It doesn’t just move forward. It reaches back and we have the power to be a portal for the past and the future, come on now! We as the cycle breakers are the chosen ones, who chose this very path. 

My mother made the contract to bring forth an ascended master to do the things that I can now do on this planet. I’ll be honest with you, It took me most of my adult life to heal this part of myself, but when I did, it was as if I had bust the shackles of eons of trauma and I was able to move forward into the Divine purpose. This took prayer and meditation. Prayer for the release of the anger at first and then the ability to be able to forgive. Over time my heart opened enough to be able to allow space and grace for another. Because I felt my anger was justified. I fought this for a long time until finally, I got tired of being angry and having no peace within myself.  I was always playing the victim to anybody who would listen wanting them to see my side and to feel sorry for me. I was careful not to tell anybody that would tell me to get over it and do the work already.

Today’s writing was inspired by the comment above, and I hope it touched somebody on a deep inner level who’s ready for that level of healing so that they can start to truly manifest a life beyond their wildest dreams beyond their ancestor’s wildest dreams and for this writing, I’m specifically speaking of the mother and father relationship. I’m not talking about forgiving the whole damn world.

I often think back to the fact that my ancestors never judged me or pushed me on this, they knew this was something that I would have to come to on my own. To hear me speak these words and write these words makes my ancestors beyond proud of me and I am beyond grateful for them, guiding me to this place of reconnection to divine source energy, healing, and connection with my ancestral lineage, including the ancestors returned that are still living today. My family is a work in progress. I am always growing and evolving, but I have to always and forever give thanks for all of my ancestors part in my journey and getting me to this place. 

This message was straight from the spirit and the heart, as soon as I got the message I got to writing and I hope that this helps someone to have a different experience with this whenever you’re ready, as for me I respect the portal that gave me life. No one on this planet has given me a bigger gift than my parents. I couldn’t possibly fully begin to heal the generational trauma and pass it forward with a resentment-filled relationship with my parents about things they did to me when I was a kid. I’m a grown woman now, I have adult children of my own. I have a grandbaby! It was time for me to let that story go and to heal deeply! I simply could not let another lifetime go by without fulfilling, my purpose, completely, and fully honoring my spiritual contract and so it is!

Today when I think of my parents and I speak about my parents, I speak life into them just like I do my ascended and transcended ancestors. I think about the gifts and the attributes and the good things that I’ve experienced with them and the gifts that I’ve grown to know. I no longer function under the story of trauma and abuse. This is Healing at one of its highest forms because most people’s trauma starts with Mama and Daddy as the roots. They are our foundational relationships in life and if things go drastically wrong there, then we create these patterns and behaviors that we take throughout life to learn how to survive and when something doesn’t go right, we end up in therapy and we blame mommy and daddy. I’m grateful I don’t have to live that way today and I’m grateful that while my parents have some life left on this planet in earth I get a chance to actively participate in restoring positive karma, the continuance of healing lifetimes of trauma for us both and creating new happy memories with the people, I am blessed to call my parents.

Once again, this is for mamas and daddy’s not the general population because they are not our ancestors. In regards to whether these people are still abusers themselves, it’s always best to use intuition, common sense, and healed boundaries. Healed people set healthy boundaries, but my experience has been that my healing has been contagious and it starts to affect those same parents differently or maybe it’s just my perspective and the way that I’m seeing things from healed eyes. This allows me to show up in a manner that allows for open-heart connections to happen instead of the trauma-filled conversation that used to dominate our connections. Because I love myself I don’t interact with people that abuse me today!

Remember, forgiveness is not about the other person, forgiveness sets you free and relinquishes the power from the other person that they have over you when you’re unforgiving. Forgiveness also does not mean your relationship with them will be instantly restored. It just means that you’re free to live a life of emotional freedom and not in bondage of traumatic events that happened to you.

As always I hope my personal experience resonates with someone. If it’s for you take what you need and leave the rest.

Zipporah Banyay

11-3-2024
