Holistic and Spiritual AF is committed to making this website welcoming and usable for everyone, including people with disabilities. Healing spaces should be open to all of us.

## Our standard

We aim to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) at Level AA.

## What we’ve done

- Text and background colors are chosen for readable contrast
- Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to content” link
- Images have text descriptions, and icon buttons have labels for screen readers
- Pages use clear headings and landmarks so assistive technology can navigate them
- The layout adapts to phones, tablets and zoom up to 200%
- Links that leave this site open in a new tab and say so to screen readers

## Known limitations

Some features are provided by other services — Cal.com (booking), Stripe (payment), Eventbrite (tickets), Gumroad (courses), MailerLite (newsletter), YouTube and Podbean (media). We choose providers that support accessibility, but we don’t control their pages. Podcast episodes do not yet all have transcripts; we are working on adding them.

## Tell us about a barrier

If anything on this site is hard to use, please let us know and we’ll do our best to help and to fix it. Send us a message through our [contact form](/contact) with the page and what happened. We aim to reply within 5 business days.
