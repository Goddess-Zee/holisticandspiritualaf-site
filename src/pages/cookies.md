We honor your digital boundaries. Cookies help us keep things running smoothly and learn what serves our community best. Nothing creepy, nothing sold — just clarity and care.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Some are needed for a site to work; others remember your choices or help us understand how the site is used.

## The cookies and storage we use

| Type | Who sets it | Why | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Your cookie choice | Holistic and Spiritual AF | Remembers whether you chose “I’m In,” “Necessary Only” or “No Thank You” so we don’t ask again | Always (stored in your browser, not a cookie we can read) |
| Email sign-up | MailerLite | Loads our newsletter sign-up form and measures sign-ups | Only after you choose “I’m In,” or when you click a sign-up button |
| Booking and payment | Cal.com and Stripe | Schedules and processes consultations | Only on their own pages, after you click a booking button |
| Tickets and courses | Eventbrite, Gumroad, Amazon | Sells class tickets, courses and books | Only on their own sites, after you click through |
| Embedded media | YouTube, Podbean | Plays videos and podcast episodes | Only when you choose to play them |

We do not use advertising cookies, and we never sell your data.

## Your three choices

- **I’m In:** necessary storage plus our newsletter tool (MailerLite) loads on every page.
- **Necessary Only:** only what the site needs to work — remembering your choice. Optional tools stay off until you click a sign-up, booking or ticket button.
- **No Thank You:** we turn off everything optional and keep only your choice itself, so we don’t ask again.

## Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page. You can also clear or block cookies in your browser settings; some features, such as the newsletter pop-up, may not work without them.
